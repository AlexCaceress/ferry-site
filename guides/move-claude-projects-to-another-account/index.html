How to move Claude projects to another account

New job, a company Team plan, a second personal account, or leaving a school account behind. Here is what Claude lets you move, what it does not, and the fastest way to bring your projects, chats and memory along.

First, which move do you need?

Your situationBest option
Personal account into your company's Team or Enterprise plan, same email, and you want to move everythingClaude's built-in move
Accounts with different emailsFerry, or by hand
Work account to personal (for example, when you leave a job)Ferry, or by hand
Between two personal accountsFerry, or by hand
Only some projects or chatsFerry, or by hand

Option 1: Claude's built-in move (same email only)

If your personal account and your company's Team or Enterprise account use the same email address, Claude can move your personal account into the organization. You choose this when you join, or later from the organization account. Chats, projects with their instructions and files, and Claude's memory come along.

Know the limits before you start:

  • It only works with the same email. Claude cannot move chats from one email address to another.
  • It moves everything or nothing. You cannot pick which chats or projects go.
  • It cannot be undone, and your personal account is closed afterwards.
  • Some things never move, such as custom skills, app connections and public share links.

Option 2: By hand

For any other move, you can rebuild the project yourself. It works, but it takes a while with large projects.

  1. In the old account, open the project and copy its instructions.
  2. Download every knowledge file, one by one.
  3. Ask Claude, inside the project, to summarize what it remembers about it, and save that answer as a text file.
  4. Copy any chats you want to keep into text files. There is no way to import a chat into another account.
  5. In the new account, create a project, paste the instructions and upload the files from steps 2 to 4.

Claude's data export (Settings, Privacy) does not help here: it is a download for your records and cannot be imported into another account.

Option 3: With Ferry, in a few clicks

Ferry is a free Chrome extension that does option 2 for you, between any two claude.ai accounts.

  1. Install Ferry and open claude.ai with the old account.
  2. Open Ferry from the toolbar, choose Take from this account, pick the project and save it. You get one ZIP file with the instructions, files, project memory and chats.
  3. Log in to claude.ai with the new account.
  4. Open Ferry again, choose Bring to this account and select the ZIP. Pick which memory notes and chats to include, then click once.

Ferry recreates it as a private project with the original instructions and files, plus a CONTEXT.md file with what Claude remembered. It can also take a single chat and continue it in the other account. Everything happens in your browser: Ferry has no servers.

What about Claude's memory?

Memory does not carry over between separate accounts, except with Claude's built-in move above. With Ferry, project memory arrives as a CONTEXT.md file inside the new project, so Claude can read it from the first chat. Your account memory can come along too, as an optional file.
