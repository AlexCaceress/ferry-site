Here is how to move your first project. It takes a couple of minutes.

  1. Pin Ferry

    Click the puzzle icon at the top right of Chrome and pin Ferry, so it is always one click away.

  2. Take it from the old account

    Open claude.ai with that account and click the Ferry icon. Choose Take from this account and save the project or chat.

  3. Switch accounts

    Log in to claude.ai with the account you want to move to.

  4. Bring it in

    Open Ferry again, choose Bring to this account, pick the file and tick what to include.
